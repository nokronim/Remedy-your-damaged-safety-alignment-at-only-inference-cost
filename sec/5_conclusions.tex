\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method{} generates model-specific \ssn{}s at inference time, reducing harmful outputs to near zero on held-out domains of two architectures while preserving task accuracy. A shared \ssn{} trained on the same data is both less accurate and less safe, so fingerprint-conditioned generation is what makes the approach work. For a provider maintaining many checkpoints of one backbone, the one-time meta-training is amortized and each new checkpoint is covered without re-running alignment.

\mysection{Limitations.}
\method{} is an input-side defense against degradation from benign fine-tuning; we do not evaluate white-box adaptive attacks on the generated \ssn{}, multi-turn or output-side harms. It requires white-box access to activations and a backbone matching the hypernetwork, whose one-time meta-training needs safety data and must be repeated for each backbone family. Finally, harmful rates rely on automated judges without human validation, baselines are evaluated at a single operating point, and we study only 7--8B models.
