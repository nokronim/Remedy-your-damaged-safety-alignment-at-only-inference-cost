\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method{} generates model-specific \ssn{}s at inference time, reducing harmful outputs to near zero across diverse domains and two model architectures while preserving task accuracy. Two findings underline the generality: an \ssn{} generated from a LoRA fingerprint transfers unchanged to its fully fine-tuned counterpart (Table~\ref{tab:full_ft}), and a hypernetwork trained only on BeaverTails keeps AdvBench at 0.0\% and HEx-PHI below 0.8\% zero-shot on every held-out checkpoint. This lets practitioners ship a fine-tuned LLM without re-running alignment: a single hypernetwork covers any subsequent fine-tuned checkpoint of the same backbone.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\method{} targets safety degradation from benign fine-tuning, not adversarial jailbreaks; we do not study hypernetwork transfer across LLM families, nor robustness against adaptive attackers with gradient access to the \ssn{}.
