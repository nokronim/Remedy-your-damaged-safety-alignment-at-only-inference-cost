\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method{} generates model-specific \ssn{}s at inference time, reducing harmful outputs to near zero across diverse domains and two model architectures while preserving task accuracy. Two findings underline the generality of the approach: an \ssn{} generated from a LoRA fingerprint transfers unchanged to its fully fine-tuned counterpart (Table~\ref{tab:full_ft}), and a hypernetwork trained only on BeaverTails harmful prompts drops AdvBench and HEx-PHI harmful rates to $\leq$0.3\% zero-shot. Limitations: we evaluate on standard benchmarks but not on adaptive attackers with white-box knowledge of the \ssn{} architecture; and we have not tested whether the hypernetwork generalizes across unseen backbone architectures, since each architecture is paired with its own hypernetwork in our experiments.
