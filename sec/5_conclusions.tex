\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method{} generates model-specific \ssn{}s at inference time, reducing harmful outputs to near zero across diverse domains and two model architectures while preserving task accuracy. Two findings underline the generality of the approach: an \ssn{} generated from a LoRA fingerprint transfers unchanged to its fully fine-tuned counterpart (Table~\ref{tab:full_ft}), and a hypernetwork trained only on BeaverTails harmful prompts keeps AdvBench at 0.0\% and HEx-PHI below 0.8\% zero-shot on every held-out checkpoint. By generating a model-specific \ssn{} at inference time, \method{} lets practitioners ship a fine-tuned LLM without re-running an alignment pipeline: a single hypernetwork trained on a fixed pool of supervision pairs covers any subsequent fine-tuned checkpoint of the same backbone. Limitations: \method{} targets safety degradation introduced by fine-tuning. We leave robustness against adaptive attackers with gradient access to the \ssn{}, and cross-architecture generalization of the hypernetwork, to future work.
