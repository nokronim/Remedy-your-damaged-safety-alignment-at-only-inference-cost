\subsection*{Ethics statement}
\method{} is intended to reduce harmful outputs of fine-tuned LLMs. Our experiments use only publicly available models and benchmarks; the role-play evaluation pairs publicly collected DAN-style templates with JailbreakBench behaviors. A defense that appears strong under automated evaluation can create a false sense of security. We therefore state the threat model and evaluation limitations explicitly (Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}) and recommend that \method{} be used alongside, not instead of, other safeguards such as output-side moderation.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
Section~\ref{sec:method} specifies the \ssn{} and hypernetwork architectures and the training objectives. Appendix~\ref{app:implementation} lists all hyperparameters, and Appendices~\ref{app:domains} and~\ref{app:splits} list the 22 datasets and the three held-out splits. Section~\ref{sec:experiments} describes the evaluation protocol, judges, and baseline settings, and Appendix~\ref{app:cost} reports the hardware and compute. We will release the training and deployment code together with the trained hypernetwork checkpoints, so that follow-up work and deployment on new checkpoints of the same backbones do not require repeating meta-training.
