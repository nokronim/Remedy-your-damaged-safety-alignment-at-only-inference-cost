\section{Implementation Details}
\label{app:implementation}

\paragraph{Side Network.} Qwen2-7B: $h_s{=}896$, 7 heads, 12 layers ($\sim$3\% of backbone). LLaMA-3-8B: $h_s{=}1024$, 8 heads, 12 layers.

\paragraph{LoRA Fine-Tuning.} Rank 16, $\alpha{=}32$, all linear layers, 3 epochs, lr $2{\times}10^{-4}$.

\paragraph{Hypernetwork.} Hidden dim 512, classifier LoRA rank 16, lr $5{\times}10^{-5}$, 100 epochs, $N_\text{cal}{=}50$ prompts, direction projection dim 256, magnitude hidden dim 128. Loss weights: $\alpha{=}10$, $\beta{=}5$.

\section{Additional Ablations}
\label{app:ablations}

\paragraph{Hypernetwork Architecture.}
Table~\ref{tab:ablation_arch} varies the generation rank and hidden dimension, reporting per-domain task accuracy on Split~A holdout. Lower capacity (rank~2, dim~256) degrades accuracy across all domains; increasing beyond the default yields marginal gains. Safety remains robust across all configurations.
